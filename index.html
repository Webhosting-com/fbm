How it works

From first contact to real progress

A clear route, so young people, parents and schools always know what happens next.

  1. Referral

    A parent, school, service or the young person gets in touch.

  2. First meet

    An informal chat to get to know them and what they want.

  3. The plan

    We agree goals together and how we'll reach them.

  4. Sessions

    Regular mentoring, workshops or in-school support.

  5. Review

    We track progress, celebrate wins and plan the next step.

FAQs

Common questions

The things parents and schools usually ask first. Answers to confirm

What ages do you work with?

We mainly work with young people of secondary school age through to their early twenties. If you're not sure whether we're the right fit, get in touch and we'll talk it through.

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions can take place in school, at a community venue, or another agreed safe space. We'll agree the best option with you at the first meet.

How do you keep young people safe?

Safeguarding comes first. Our mentors are background checked, we follow a written safeguarding policy, and we keep parents, carers and schools informed in line with it.

How long does mentoring last?

Most young people start with a programme of weekly sessions. We then review progress together and decide whether to continue, change focus or finish.

Can a young person refer themselves?

Yes. Young people can get in touch directly. For under-18s we'll also speak with a parent or carer before sessions begin.

Do you work with schools and councils?

Yes. We deliver workshops and targeted 1:1 mentoring for schools, alternative provision and youth services, with progress reporting built in.
